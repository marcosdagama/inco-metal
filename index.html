01 Company

Specialists in metallurgical process engineering.

INCO is a group of companies that engineers and manufactures equipment for metallurgical processes, ferrous and non-ferrous.

Our core is flat-product (coil) transformation lines, along with casting and continuous casting for non-ferrous metals such as aluminium and copper. Years of close collaboration with clients have produced processes and machinery that exist nowhere else — and a multidisciplinary Technical Office that starts every project from the client's production targets.

  • 01Every proposal is technically and economically viable in a competitive global market.
  • 02Machinery matched precisely to the process requirements and output targets.
  • 03Process value-add that delivers a solid, measurable return on investment.

Quality policy

Quality is built into every stage, from concept to commissioning. Each project goes through multidisciplinary review to secure technical excellence and long-term equipment reliability.

02 Capabilities

The full lifecycle of a process line.

From the first feasibility study to operator training, one engineering team carries the project through.

01

Coil processing lines

Painting, pickling, annealing, levelling and tension-levelling lines for aluminium, steel and copper coils — for architecture, appliances and electronics.

02

Rolling mills

Reversible cold mills, mini-mills and skin-pass mills for steel, aluminium, copper and special alloys. Widths up to 1,800 mm, speeds up to 1,500 m/min.

03

Casting & continuous casting

Melting, casting and continuous-casting sections for aluminium and copper, including hot-rolling tandems and billet plants.

04

Custom engineering

Our Technical Office designs bespoke processes and machinery that give clients a lasting strategic edge.

05

Turnkey installation

Project management from manufacturing to on-site assembly, testing and commissioning, with our engineers present throughout.

06

Training & after-sales

Operator and maintenance training from day one, ongoing technical support and guaranteed spare-parts availability.

04 Service

From first study to full production.

One team, accountable from the initial assessment to the line running at nominal speed.

  1. Phase 01

    Feasibility

    Analysis of the product, targets and site; layout and a technical-economic proposal.

    → Proposal & layout
  2. Phase 02

    Engineering

    Process, mechanical, electrical and automation engineering by our Technical Office.

    → Design approval
  3. Phase 03

    Manufacturing

    Fabrication and machining with supplier quality control and factory acceptance tests.

    → FAT
  4. Phase 04

    Installation

    Assembly at the client's plant, led and supervised by INCO engineers.

    → Mechanical completion
  5. Phase 05

    Commissioning

    Cold and hot tests, ramp-up to nominal speed and performance verification.

    → SAT · acceptance
  6. Phase 06

    Training & support

    Operator and maintenance training, spare parts, remote and on-site support.

    → Continuous
